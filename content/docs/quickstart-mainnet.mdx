Whitechain mainnet is chain `1875`. **There is currently nothing to settle on it.**

## Why

This facilitator serves exactly one stablecoin: **USDT**. Gas is always paid in WBT by our signer.

Mainnet USDT at `0xF95604a4034d8407d3F1256De56C9ae37F299cb8` is an older `TetherToken` with `permit` but no `transferWithAuthorization`, so a gasless x402 `exact` payment is impossible against it. Its total supply is also zero. [Supported assets](/docs/supported-assets) has the on-chain evidence.

Any mainnet `/verify` or `/settle` call is refused with `unsupported_asset`.

## What to do instead

Build and test on [Sepolia](/docs/quickstart-testnet), where USDT supports EIP-3009. Mainnet support arrives when a mainnet USDT with EIP-3009 is deployed; your integration then changes by the network id and asset address only.
