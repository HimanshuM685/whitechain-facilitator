This is a free public service that pays gas out of its own wallet, so it has to have limits. They exist to stop one caller draining the gas wallet everyone shares — not to meter you.

Nothing is ever broadcast when a limit refuses a request, so a `429` or `503` costs you nothing but a retry.

## Rate limits

Counted per 60-second window. The first one you exceed is the one you hit.

| Scope | Default | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Per client IP | 120 / min | Blunt instrument against a loop |
| Per payer address | 30 / min | One agent cannot crowd out the rest |
| Per `payTo` (merchant) | 120 / min | One merchant cannot either |
| Facilitator-wide | 1200 / min | Bounds our RPC fan-out |

A refusal is `429` with a `Retry-After` header in seconds. Honour it rather than retrying immediately.

## Minimum payment

**0.01** of the asset — `amount: "10000"` at six decimals.

Below that, the WBT we spend on gas exceeds the value being transferred, which makes the payment a net loss for the network and a free gas drain for us. Payments under the floor are rejected with `amount_below_minimum`.

There is no maximum. The amount is in the payer's signature, so it is bounded by what they chose to sign.

## Gas

There is no daily gas budget. Settlement is limited only by the facilitator's WBT balance; below the operating floor `/settle` returns `503 signer_balance_low`. Check it:

<NetCode lang="bash">
{`curl -s {{facilitatorUrl}}/health | jq '.networks[] | {network, status, balanceWbt, estimatedSettlesLeft}'`}
</NetCode>

## Per-settlement gas cap

Each settlement is capped at **300,000 gas**. A normal EIP-3009 transfer uses 70,000–90,000, so this only bites on something pathological — which is the point: a single request cannot cost us an unbounded amount.

## Mainnet kill switch

Mainnet settlement can be switched off. When it is:

- `/verify` keeps working, so you can validate an integration without us spending anything
- `/settle` returns `503` with `mainnet_disabled`
- `/health` reports that network as `status: "disabled"`

Testnet is unaffected. If you need mainnet to be reliably on, [run your own instance](/docs/self-host).

## Low-balance refusal

If our signer's WBT drops below the operating floor, `/settle` returns `503` with `signer_balance_low` rather than broadcasting a transaction that would fail. `/health` reports `degraded`. This is a failure we would rather hand you cleanly than pretend about.

## What is not limited

- `/supported`, `/assets`, `/health` and the log endpoints are not rate limited beyond the per-IP ceiling.
- `/verify` is included in the counts, but it spends no gas — call it as much as the rate limit allows.

## If the limits do not fit

They are all environment variables on a service you can run yourself. [Self-host](/docs/self-host): same code, your signer. Your merchant config changes by exactly one URL.
